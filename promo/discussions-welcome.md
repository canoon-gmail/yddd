# Discussion post draft (pin it in "Announcements")

**Title:** Welcome — tell us what you made / what was hard

---

Hi, and thank you for trying YDDD!

YDDD is a small, simple 3D CAD app for making 3D-print parts. You draw a shape, push and pull it, and get exact sizes in mm. No history tree, no long setup.

It is new, and I make it alone. So your words help a lot — even one line.

**Please reply here with any of these:**

1. **What did you make?** A screenshot or a photo of the print is great.
2. **What was hard?** Where did you get stuck, or what did not do what you thought?
3. **What do you want to make next** that YDDD can not do yet?

Short is fine. Bad news is fine too — "I gave up after 5 minutes because ..." is very useful.

**Other places:**

- Something broke or gave a wrong shape? → [Bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (a saved `.yddd` file helps — zip it first)
- Just tried it for the first time? → [First impressions](../../issues/new?template=first_impressions.yml) (3 short questions)
- A part you can not make yet? → [Feature request](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
- A question? → the **Q&A** category

**Tips to start:** press **F1** in the app for all keys. **Help** menu has examples (Bird House, Cable Storage Box, Planter ...) you can open and change.

Thank you!

# Suggested Discussion categories

Keep it few. Delete the GitHub defaults you do not need.

| Category | Format | What goes there |
|---|---|---|
| Announcements | Announcement (only the author posts) | New versions, this welcome post (pinned) |
| Show what you made | Open discussion | Screenshots, prints, `.yddd` files to share |
| Q&A | Question / Answer | "How do I ...?" — answers can be marked as the answer |
| Ideas | Open discussion | Rough ideas before they become a feature request issue |
| General | Open discussion | Everything else |

Notes:
- The relative links above (`../../issues/new?template=...`) work from a Discussion page; if not, use the full `https://github.com/canoon-gmail/yddd/issues/new?template=...`.
- "Show what you made" is the most important one: it shows new visitors real parts, and tells us what people really use.
